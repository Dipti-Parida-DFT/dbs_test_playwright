5. Learnings Captured

  • Verify User: testData.AccountTransfer.verifyUserId ("SPI307A17") does NOT work for re-login in SIT — use testData.ManagePayroll.SIT.loginUserIdUser2 ("DBSAUTO0001") instead.
  • Verify Tab Search: Requires hardWait(page) before entering reference in transactionFilter — otherwise filter may not register input.
  • Post-verify Status: After verification through Approval menu, ACT payment transitions from "Pending Verification" → "Pending Approval" in Transfer Center.
  • Delete Pattern: Post-verify delete uses deleteOpenPayeeOrReferenceNo() + transactionDeletedPopupOkButton dismiss (same as TC09/TC10).
